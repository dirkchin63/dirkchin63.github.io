HKIME practice word banks v1. Independently assembled practice material, not official competition questions.
Chinese: Jieba dictionary 0.42.1 (https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba, MIT); words selected by POS and frequency. Hong Kong traditional forms converted with OpenCC (https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC, Apache-2.0) and deduplicated.
English: lowercase WordNet 3.0 lemmas (Princeton University, enclosed license), ranked using wordfreq 3.1.1 (Robyn Speer and contributors, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq). The frequency data and the derived English selection are credited under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/. WordNet definitions and frequency values are not included.
